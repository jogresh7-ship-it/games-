# Sonic the Hedgehog: Beta Prototype

Double-click `index.html` to play. No server or install needed.

**Controls:** Arrows or WASD to move · Down to roll · Space, Z or X to jump · Enter to pause. On phones and tablets, buttons appear on screen.

**In this beta:** Green Hill Zone Acts 1 and 2, rings, Motobugs, Crabmeats, Buzz Bombers, monitors (rings, shield, 1-up), springs, spikes, the end-of-act signpost and score tally.

**Known rough edges:** the loop and S-tubes are scenery only, collision on some edges may be off, there's no music yet, and the continue and title screens are placeholders.
