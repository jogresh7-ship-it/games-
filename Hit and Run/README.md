# Hit & Run: Maple Springs

An original open-world driving game. Double-click `index.html` to play.

Drive Hank (an original character) around the town of Maple Springs. Finish six missions, collect coins, and smash crates, cones, bins, mailboxes and hydrants. Smashing things and ramming cars fills the **Hit & Run meter**. When it's full, the cops chase you. If they stay on your bumper too long you're **BUSTED** and fined coins. Get far away to let the meter drain and lose them.

**Controls:** Arrows or WASD to drive, Space for a handbrake drift, H for the horn, Enter to pause. On phones and tablets, buttons appear on screen.

**Missions:** Breakfast Run, Late for Work, Coin Collector, Spring Cleaning, Checkpoint Rally, The Big Getaway.
