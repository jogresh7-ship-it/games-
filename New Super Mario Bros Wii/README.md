# New Super Mario Bros. Wii (fan prototype)

Double-click `index.html` to play.

**Controls:** Left/Right arrows to move, Space or Z to jump (hold for higher), Shift or X to run, Enter to pause. On the title screen, Left/Right picks Mario, Luigi (jumps highest), Blue Toad, Yellow Toad (a little faster), Peach (hold jump to float) or Yoshi (hold jump to flutter). On phones and tablets, buttons appear on screen.

**In this prototype:** World 1-1 to 1-3 using the grassland tiles and background, ? blocks (coins, one 1-up), breakable bricks, coins, 3 Star Coins per course, pipes, a goal pole and a COURSE CLEAR screen.

**Characters** are rendered from the 3D models (idle, walk, jump, fall, death). **Placeholder:** the enemy is a made-up spiky ball until real enemy art is added. Sounds are simple made-up beeps.
