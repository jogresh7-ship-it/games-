# New Super Mario Bros. Wii (fan prototype)

Double-click `index.html` to play.

**Controls:** Left/Right arrows to move, Space or Z to jump (hold for higher), Shift or X to run, Enter to pause. On the title screen, Left/Right picks Mario or Luigi (Luigi jumps higher). On phones and tablets, buttons appear on screen.

**In this prototype:** World 1-1 to 1-3 using the grassland tiles and background, ? blocks (coins, one 1-up), breakable bricks, coins, 3 Star Coins per course, pipes, a goal pole and a COURSE CLEAR screen.

**Placeholders:** the sheets had no Mario body sprites or enemies, so the player is the Mario/Luigi face icon and the enemy is a made-up spiky ball. Send sprite sheets for Mario and Goombas and they'll be swapped in. Sounds are simple made-up beeps.
