# Minecraft: Pocket Edition (fan prototype)

Double-click `index.html` to play. Needs a browser with WebGL (any modern one).

Pick **Creative** (fly with a double-tap of Space, unlimited blocks) or **Survival** (health, fall damage, drowning). Each world is new: hills, beaches, lakes, trees, cacti, caves and ores.

**Controls:** WASD to move, mouse to look (click the game to grab the mouse), left click to break, right click to place, 1–9 or the mouse wheel to pick a block, E for the next page of blocks, Space to jump, Shift to sneak or fly down, Esc to pause. On phones and tablets: drag to look, with on-screen buttons for moving, jumping, breaking, placing and flying.

Block textures, the hotbar and the logo come from the Pocket Edition sprite sheets.
