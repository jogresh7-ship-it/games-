# Minecraft: Pocket Edition (fan prototype)

Double-click `index.html` to play. Needs a browser with WebGL (any modern one).

**Screens, styled after Pocket Edition:** Mojang splash → title (with a yellow splash message) → **Start Game** → **Select world** (your saved worlds) → **Create New World** (name, seed, Creative or Survival) → *Generating world*. In game there's a pause button (top right), a game menu (Back to game / Options / Quit to title), an inventory (the "..." button next to the hotbar, or E), Options (sound, sensitivity, invert Y, auto-jump, render distance) and a "You died!" screen.

**Gameplay:** new worlds have hills, beaches, deserts, snow peaks, lakes, trees, caves and ores. Creative: instant breaking, every block, double-tap jump to fly. Survival: blocks take time to mine (with crack animation), drop into your inventory with counts, hearts, fall damage, drowning and health regen. Worlds save automatically in your browser.

**Controls:** WASD to move, mouse to look (click to grab the mouse), hold left click to mine, right click to place, middle click to pick a block, 1–9 or the wheel to change slots, E for inventory, Q to drop, Space to jump, Shift to sneak, Ctrl to sprint, Esc to pause.
**Touch:** Pocket Edition d-pad with jump in the middle, drag to look, tap to place, hold to mine.

The block textures, buttons, hotbar, font, logos and d-pad come from the Pocket Edition sprite sheets.
